# Balance tools

Headless AI-vs-AI battles for tuning enemies against the strength model (`src/game/power.ts`). Nothing here ships
with the game. Reports go to `tools/balance/out/` (gitignored).

## The model

- A fighter's **value** is `sqrt(effective HP × damage per turn)`: Health stretched by Defense, and the best attack
  stat cut by the other side's Defense after penetration. Enemy types multiply it by their `power` (in
  `enemies.json`, fitted here) for what their stats don't show.
- A group's **power** is `N^k × (average value)²` with `k = strength.numbersExponent` (1.75, fitted by `fitlaws.py`
  against the square law, the linear law, ranged/melee splits and crowding caps). The **power ratio** of two groups
  says who should win; `strength.winCurve` maps it to an expected **score**.
- **Score**: a win earns 1, a loss the share of enemies defeated, less `HEALTH_WEIGHT` (0.5) × the share of the
  party's Health lost (the game's after-battle loss: each member's share of HP at its lowest, all of it for
  falling); 0 to 1 (`sim.ts`).
- The **expected party** (`src/game/reference.ts`, `POWER` in `power.ts`): Guardian, Caster, Archer, then Cleric
  (from level 8) and Bruiser (from level 13), talent spreads averaging B, average gear for the level (10% / 5% / 2%
  a level for at least Common / at least Fine / Masterwork; simulated parties roll each piece).
- A quest's **threat** is the log of its expected enemies' power against a four-member reference party of matching
  strength; **skulls** come from threat through `strength.skulls` points: half a skull where random level-1 trios
  score about 90% (the model's 98.5%, `skulls.sim.ts`), ten where the five-member level-20 party scores 50%, and
  points between so the typical quest of levels 5, 10 and 15 shows 2.5, 5 and 7.5 skulls.
- Quests are generated by skulls (`board.ts enemiesForSkulls`): a type and level whose usual group (`group`) is
  about that dangerous, its companions, and a group of 75–133% of the usual one.

## Running

```bash
npm run balance -- --name grid --seeds 40 --level 4,8,12 --stageOffset -2,0,2 --count 3,5,7 --quality expected
```

Every list flag is a comma list and the job is their cross product (`jobs.ts`); `--workers` defaults to the CPU
count; `--random <seeds>` fields random guild-hall recruits instead of the reference builds. Plans:
`--plan types` (each enemy type alone at the bottom, middle and top of its levels, at power ratios around its level's
target), `--plan levels` (the expected party of each level against quests at its typical skulls), `--plan ranks`
(against quests posted for its rank), `--plan skulls --stage <skulls>` (quests at given skulls, for the anchors) and
`--plan board`. Each report records the model's prediction per cell (`meta.ts`).

- `python3 tools/balance/checkranks.py <report>`: score against the target, per level or per skulls.
- `python3 tools/balance/lengths.py <report>`: battle length per enemy type.
- `python3 tools/balance/fitlaws.py <report> [...]`: compare laws of numbers and fit the curve.
- `npx vitest run --config tools/balance/vitest.config.ts tools/balance/<name>.sim.ts` for the probes: `skulls`
  (anchors and skull points, writes guild.json), `campaign` (pace: XP pool, renown thresholds, gold per rank),
  `questmix` (the board's mix and variety per skull), `burst`, `rivals`, `replay` (one battle, turn by turn).

## Rebalancing enemies

`tools/balance/iterate.sh <first> <iterations> [seeds]` loops: simulate each type alone (`--plan types`), fit the
curve, a power factor per type and level and a tempo per type and level (`fitfac.py`), then rescale the stats
(`rebalance.sim.ts`): at every level of each type's range, the expected party should score `targets.json` (99% at
level 1, 70% at 5, 60% at 10, 50% at 20) against the type's usual group; base stats, `growthPerLevel` and a
`growthBend` are fitted by least squares. Health is traded against attack (same value) so fights average about 10
rounds early and 11 late (Trolls 14). Limits: more of the strength goes into attack late (`attackShare`); M.Atk that
only heals stays at its original value; the tempo stays within ×0.4–×2.5 of each type's original Health-to-attack
balance (`enemies.orig.json`); one basic hit takes at most 35% of the frailest reference Adventurer's effective
Health; corrections are damped (`BALANCE_GAIN`, 0.6). After the loop, copy the last fit's `a`, `b` into
`strength.winCurve`, then run `skulls` and `campaign`.
